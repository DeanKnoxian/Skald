# Starting your Skald character

After Wabbajack reports a successful installation, open the included `ModOrganizer.exe`. Choose the **Skald** profile and **Skald** executable, then press **Run**. The Skald entry launches SKSE from the installed setup.

## Begin a new game

Choose **New** and complete character creation. Skald uses **Skyrim Unbound Reborn** for starting choices. Its starting-menu shortcut is **Enter** on keyboard; you can also open **System > Mod Configuration > Skyrim Unbound**. Follow that menu's prompts to choose your start and begin your adventure.

The starting menu does not automatically open in the supplied settings. If you are waiting at the starting stage after character creation, use Enter or open Skyrim Unbound in Mod Configuration. Give the new character's mod menus time to initialize before making further changes.

Use a new save for Skald. Keep saves from other modlists separate, and keep the installed mod and plugin setup together throughout your playthrough.

## Choosing the Frosthavn path

Frosthavn is an optional start. In Skyrim Unbound's presets, choose **Frosthavn - Dawnstar** if you want that path, then follow the starting prompts. You may choose an ordinary Unbound start instead.

On the Frosthavn path, follow the journal and speak with **Runa at the Windpeak Inn in Dawnstar** about the deeds ahead. Her dialogue explains where to begin; the original quests still have their own prerequisites. Return to her as your journal directs. Later, her dream-draught leads into a vision where **Tsun** gives lessons and trials. Use his farewell when you are ready to return.

Frosthavn's **Recommended** perk option spends eligible unspent points toward its intended build. You can choose **Manual** instead. Its quest directions, recommendation prompt, trial notifications and earned blessing options are available under **System > Mod Configuration > Frosthavn**.

## Away - Come Away

Skald's courier start becomes eligible after **five elapsed in-game days or reaching level 5**, whichever comes first. When the courier delivers the letter, **read it** to begin the quest. The time or level threshold enables delivery; it does not replace reading the letter.

## Learn the controls

Read [CONTROLS.md](CONTROLS.md) before combat. Important controller differences include **B to sneak**, **X for the saved dodge binding**, **LB + Y to draw or sheath**, **LB + A to shout**, **D-pad Up for Favorites**, and **LB + B for Wheeler**. The controller's **View** button opens the character menu; **Menu** opens the journal.

The controller is enabled in the supplied preferences. Keyboard and mouse remain available. Mod Configuration contains individual mod settings; saved characters can retain their own control choices, so check a mod's menu if a shortcut differs from the guide.

For launch or installation problems, see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). The final installer remains pending; use the current Main File description on [Skald's Nexus page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/193977) when it becomes available.
